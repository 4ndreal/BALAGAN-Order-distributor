How it works: Paste your order list below (one item per line, English or Indonesian — e.g. "Baby Potato 1 kg" or "Kentang Baby 1 kg"), pick Kitchen or Bar, then click Distribute. Kitchen items route by cheapest active supplier plus every standing rule (named-supplier locks, pantry→Mimba, thresholds). Bar produce goes to a single cheapest full-coverage supplier (Buk Bunga is backup-only). Anything unmatched lands in a clearly flagged Undistributed pool at the bottom — assign it yourself with the dropdown before copying.
