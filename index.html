How it works: Paste your order list below (one item per line, e.g. "Baby Potato 1 kg"), pick Kitchen or Bar, then click Distribute. Each line is matched against the matrix and routed by cheapest active supplier, standing named-supplier rules, and the pantry rule. Review the routing before copying — unmatched items or low-confidence matches are flagged in orange/red so you can fix them with the dropdown before sending.

Search or sort the full supplier matrix. Disabled suppliers (SM, Wika, MKS, MKU, Agus) are shown for reference but excluded from auto-routing.
